Guard Mobile

Family rules for their Android device.

Guard Mobile is the child app for Android phones and tablets. Pair it with your parent account to apply app rules, website restrictions, time limits and schedules.

Android child appDashboard or Android Controller

Controls depend on the right permissions.

Website rules

A local Android VPN service applies site restrictions and internet lockout rules. Android must allow the VPN to stay active.

App use & limits

Accessibility and usage access support foreground app detection, app blocking, screen-time reporting and per-app limits.

Locks & requests

Lock overlays show bedtime, time-limit and parent lock states. Children can ask for more time or access.

Set up together.

  1. Contact us for the current Android child build and device-specific setup guidance.
  2. Open the child app and use the pairing code currently shown to add it in your parent dashboard or Android Controller.
  3. Complete the required Android permission steps: VPN, accessibility, usage access, overlay and device administration where prompted.
  4. Check protection and connection status before relying on a schedule or app rule. Review battery/background settings if reporting stops.

Android manufacturers can restrict background services. Disabled permissions or a stopped service can limit enforcement. Another VPN can conflict with the app’s VPN, so check your device setup.

Guard Mobile runs on the child’s device. Guard Controller is a separate Android app for the parent. The current lineup does not include an iOS child app, location tracking, or a Mac child app.
