Layers Student

A little help, right on the page.

Layers Student adds learning tools to supported websites in Chrome and Edge. Use it independently for study, or join a teacher’s room for classroom activities.

Contact for extension accessClassroom setup
Chrome & EdgeFree student extension

Read, understand, and keep going.

Dictionary & translation

Define words, find synonyms, and translate selections. Dictionary and translation queries use online services.

Read aloud

Listen to selected text, a paragraph, or an article. Choose an available browser voice and reading speed.

Notes & selection tools

Keep a scratchpad and open learning actions from selected text. Preferences and notes are stored in your browser.

Speech translation

Use push-to-talk where browser speech recognition is available. Microphone permission and a connection are needed.

Classroom focus

Join a room to receive the teacher’s links, focus settings, blocked-site rules and schedules. Send a help request when you need assistance.

PDF reading

Open supported PDFs through the extension’s included viewer. Browser-internal pages and some protected pages cannot host the toolbar.

Layers Student is a learning extension. The separate Layers Guard browser extension is for local parent controls. Guard Family’s Windows and Android device apps are different again.
