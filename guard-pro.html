Questions before you start?

Is Guard Family the same as the free extension?

No. Guard Family uses paired Windows and Android child apps with a parent dashboard. The free browser Guard extension keeps its settings in the browser and is managed locally.

Can I manage everything from a browser?

The web dashboard is a parent controller. Your child’s Windows or Android device still needs the corresponding app, pairing, and permissions.

Does it support Mac or iPhone child devices?

Mac apps are discontinued, and there is no iPhone/iPad child app in the current lineup. A parent can use a compatible browser on these devices to open the web dashboard.

Can protection be bypassed?

Device settings, administrator access, disabled permissions, network loss and other factors can reduce protection. Check the reported status and follow the setup guidance; Layers does not promise that any device is impossible to bypass.
