Teaching tools that stay close.

Speech translation & captions

Use push-to-talk speech recognition and translation with floating captions. Speech and translation use online services; a microphone and connection are needed.

Timer & random picker

Use countdown and stopwatch modes. Save class lists and select students at random.

Screen annotation

Draw over the screen and use a pointer while presenting. The overlay follows your lesson rather than requiring a separate whiteboard.

Dictionary & word support

Look up definitions and synonyms, or translate a word or phrase between languages.

Noise meter

Show microphone levels and set a classroom noise threshold. Results depend on your microphone and room setup.

Classroom connection

Create a room, share a code, and work with students who have the Layers Student browser extension.

Connect the class with a room code.

Create a classroom room in the Windows teacher app. Students enter the room code in Layers Student. The teacher toolbar can then send a link, apply Focus Mode or browser lockdown, maintain blocked websites, and set classroom schedules.

Students can ask for help from their toolbar. These controls act through the extension in participating browsers; they do not control the whole student computer.

A separate connection from Guard

Classroom rooms connect teachers and student browsers. Guard Family pairs child devices with a parent account. You don’t need to mix the two systems.

Access & trial behaviour

The Windows app includes a local 14-day trial. It does not collect payment details or automatically bill when the trial ends. At expiry it asks for a licence key.
